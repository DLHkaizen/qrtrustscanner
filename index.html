QR Trust Scanner

Look before you open.

English · Tiếng Việt · 简体中文 · Português (Brasil) · Español · Bahasa Indonesia · 日本語

QR Trust Scanner shows where a QR code or a link leads before you tap: the real domain in large type, what is worth a second look, and — for a payment code — who gets the money. It never opens anything by itself, and everything is checked on your iPhone.

The real domain, large

A link can put any familiar word in front of someone else's address. The app shows the real domain first.

Who are you about to pay?

For EMVCo-standard payment QR codes used in Viet Nam, Brazil, Indonesia and Malaysia: the recipient, the amount, and whether the code's check value is right.

Signals, not verdicts

Each signal is one plain sentence with a “Why?”. Three levels, and you decide.
