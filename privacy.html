Privacy

QR Trust Scanner collects no data. App Store label: Data Not Collected.

  • History stays on your device, in the app's own storage. There is no account, no developer server and no analytics.
  • Camera pictures are used only to read a code. They are not saved and not sent anywhere.
  • Links and codes you check are analysed on the device. The app never requests a link from a code, and never sends it to anyone.
  • The app downloads a public warning list (reported phishing domains) to the device, at most once a day. The download says nothing about you or what you scan; you can switch it off.
  • If you choose to check a domain's age, that domain — not the full link — is sent to the domain registry responsible for it. This happens only when you tap, and the app tells you first.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Open data we use

  • Warning list: PhishDestroy “destroylist” (github.com/phishdestroy/destroylist), MIT License, © 2019 PhishDestroy.
  • Public Suffix List (publicsuffix.org), Mozilla Public License 2.0.
  • IANA: the root zone's list of domain endings and the RDAP bootstrap registry.
