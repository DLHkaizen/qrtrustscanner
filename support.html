Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. Anything else: write to us.

What do the three levels mean?
“No warning signs seen”: nothing the app checks stood out. “Look closely before opening”: something is unusual — read the signals first. “High-risk signs”: strong signs were found, and opening needs a held press. The level comes only from the signals shown on the card.
Why does the app never say “safe”?
Because no app can know that. A link with no visible signs can still be harmful, and the app cannot see a sticker placed over another code in the real world. It shows what it noticed; you decide.
How do I check something from another app?
Tap Share on a picture, a screenshot, a link or some text, then choose QR Trust Scanner. The first time, swipe the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn it on. A short result appears right there.
How do I add the “Scan QR” control?
Open Control Center, tap +, choose Add a Control and search for QR Trust Scanner. The same control can be placed on the Lock Screen or assigned to the Action Button in Settings.
How do I restore Lifetime?
Settings → Lifetime → Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
Does Family Sharing work?
Yes. Lifetime is a one-time purchase that can be shared with your Family Sharing group, if purchase sharing is on.
How is the warning list updated?
The app carries a list of reported phishing domains and refreshes it at most once a day, preferring Wi-Fi. You can switch that off, or update by hand, in Settings → Warning list. Only the list is downloaded; what you scan is never sent.
